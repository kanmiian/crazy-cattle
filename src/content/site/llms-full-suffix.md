## Download Game
If you want a standalone PC gaming experience with offline support and dedicated GPU rendering, download the official version of Crazy Cattle 3D created by Anna Mayberry directly from itch.io (https://annamayberry.itch.io/crazy-cattle-3d).

## Game Features
Crazy Cattle 3D has many unique features:
- Physics-driven system: A momentum-based physics engine delivers unpredictable collisions and ragdoll dynamics
- Diverse environments: Three distinct battlegrounds (Ireland rolling hills, Iceland volcanic cliffs, and New Zealand alpine ridges)
- Pure skill combat: Master timing, angular momentum, and terrain slopes rather than RNG loot
- Easy to learn: Simple WASD + Space controls that reward positioning mastery
- Single-player vs AI Bots: Fast survival matches against an aggressive herd of AI sheep

## How to Play
In Crazy Cattle 3D, you'll learn how to:
- Steer your sheep with WASD / Arrow Keys and adjust perspective with the mouse
- Build downhill speed to convert momentum into high-knockout collisions
- Use Spacebar for explosive charges and jumping over rival rushes
- Exploit map edges and hazard zones to send rival AI flying off the arena

## System Requirements
For the best browser experience:
- Modern web browser (latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) with WebGL enabled
- Desktop computer with keyboard and mouse recommended for competitive control
- 4GB+ RAM and hardware graphics acceleration toggled on
